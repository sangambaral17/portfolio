04 / HOW I THINK

Make it useful.
Make it make sense.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Start with the real problem.

Listen, map the constraints, and find the part worth solving. The clearest brief is often a better starting point than more code.

02 — ENGINEER

Care about the invisible.

Data integrity, clear boundaries and maintainable systems matter just as much as what someone sees on the screen.

03 — REFINE

Keep asking better questions.

Build, inspect, test and iterate. Be honest about what works, what is uncertain and what the next version should improve.
